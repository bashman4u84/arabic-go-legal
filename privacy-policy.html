Privacy Policy

Updated: 20 October 2026 · App: Arabic Go (com.arabicgo.evolve)

This Privacy Policy explains how Al-Furqān Institute (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Arabic Go app for Android and iOS (“the App”), a fun Arabic vocabulary game for children and families. By installing or using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to make the App work and to keep it fair and fun:

  • Account information: username, email address, display name, and country, plus a securely hashed password. An email is used to confirm your account and to send password-reset links.
  • Game data: your scores, coins, runs, word-gate progress, inventory, and leaderboard position. This is used to show the world leaderboard and your personal bests.
  • Analytics device information: when gameplay analytics is on, Firebase processes app/device information and an app-instance identifier, along with usage/session information. Separate custom device/session reporting to our database remains disabled.
  • Push notifications: a device token so we can send you game notifications (daily tips, achievements). You can disable notifications any time in your device settings.
  • Ad interactions: which reward ads were watched or clicked. Reward ads are short videos (e.g. YouTube or our own hosted clips); no third-party advertising networks are used.
  • Gameplay analytics: Firebase Analytics processes events such as runs, scores, learning answers and artifact use, plus its automatic app/session events. This is enabled by default in version 1.0.0 (build 10) and can be switched off in Settings → Gameplay analytics. We do not send your email, username, password or game account ID to Firebase Analytics. Firebase uses an app-instance identifier, so this data is pseudonymous, not fully anonymous. Advertising identifiers (the Android Advertising ID and Apple’s IDFA) are not collected, advertising personalization is disabled, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

2. How we use the information

  • To create and manage your account and keep you signed in.
  • To run the game: leaderboards, scores, coins, inventory, and rewards.
  • To deliver and measure the reward-ad experience.
  • To send push notifications you opt into.
  • To understand usage (daily active users, play time, app versions) so we can improve performance and content.
  • To protect the service: prevent abuse, keep leaderboards fair, and support account security.

3. Where data is stored and who helps us

  • Supabase hosts our authentication and database (account profiles, scores, sessions, ad history). Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and passwords are stored only as salted hashes.
  • Firebase (Google) is used for push notification delivery (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and pseudonymous gameplay analytics (Firebase Analytics). Google’s privacy policy describes its processing.
  • Google Play and the Apple App Store distribute the App; their own data practices apply to downloads and the store platforms.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it except with the service providers above or where required by law.

4. Children’s privacy

The App is designed for children and families. We keep the data we collect to the minimum needed for the game to work, and we do not collect more detailed location data, chat content, or browsing activity. Parents or guardians who created an account for a child may review, correct, or delete the child’s data at any time using the contact details below.

5. Data retention & deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the App under Settings → Delete account; this takes effect immediately. You can also request deletion by email — see our Data Deletion page. We delete or anonymise data within 30 days of a verified email request.

6. Security

We use HTTPS everywhere, hashed passwords, and Supabase’s managed, access-controlled infrastructure. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information with industry-standard safeguards.

7. Your rights

  • Access & correction: view or update your profile (username, name, country) in the App.
  • Deletion: delete your account in the App (Settings → Delete account) or request removal by email (see above).
  • Notifications: turn push notifications on/off from your device settings at any time.
  • Gameplay analytics: turn collection off in the App’s Settings. This stops future gameplay analytics and resets local Firebase Analytics data and its app-instance identifier; it does not delete information already received by Google. Turning analytics off does not affect gameplay, your account, purchases or push notifications.
  • Complaints: contact us with any concern; we respond promptly.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date, and significant changes will be announced in the App.

Questions or requests? Contact us at ceo@madrasago.com — we normally reply within 2 business days.
