Data Deletion Request

Arabic Go (com.arabicgo.evolve) — you are in control of your data

You can request deletion of your Arabic Go account and all associated personal data at any time. This removes your profile, username, scores, runs, leaderboard entries, usage sessions, device records, push token, and ad history from our systems.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Arabic Go and tap the gear icon on the main menu to open Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account, then tap it again to confirm.

Your account, profile, scores, runs, leaderboard entries, coins, artifacts, inbox and push token are deleted straight away. Ad and usage records are kept only without any link to you.

Or request deletion by email

  1. Send an email to ceo@madrasago.com with the subject line “Data Deletion Request”.
  2. Include the email address and username you used to sign up (this helps us verify the request).
  3. For a child’s account, a parent or guardian may make the request on the child’s behalf.

What happens next

  • We verify the request (normally within 2 business days) to make sure only the account owner (or their guardian) can delete the data.
  • Once verified, your account and all associated data are permanently deleted within 30 days.
  • You will receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete.
  • Deletion cannot be undone — your scores and progress cannot be restored afterwards.

Other ways to control your data

  • Uninstall the app: stops new data collection on that device.
  • Notifications: disable push notifications in your device settings.
  • Signed-in data: deleting your account also removes the data stored on our servers; local game data on the device can be cleared by uninstalling the app.

Questions about this process? See our Privacy Policy or contact us.
