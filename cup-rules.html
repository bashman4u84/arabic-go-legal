Weekly Cup Rules

Updated: 13 November 2026 · App: Arabic Go (com.arabicgo.evolve)

The Arabic Go Weekly Cup is a free competition organised by Al-Furqān Institute. By taking part you agree to these rules.

1. When it runs

Each Cup runs from Sunday 8:00pm to the following Sunday 8:00pm, Lagos time (WAT, UTC+1). A new Cup starts as soon as the previous one ends.

2. Who can enter

Anyone with an Arabic Go account. Entry is free and no purchase is needed. Play a Challenge run while signed in and your score is entered automatically.

3. How the winner is decided

  • Your best single Challenge run of the week counts. Adventure and friend runs do not.
  • The runner with the highest score when the Cup ends is the champion. If two runners tie, whoever reached that score first ranks higher.
  • The champion is announced by in-app notification and email shortly after the Cup ends, and is listed under Champions in the app.

4. Prize

The champion wins one free week of advertising for their business inside Arabic Go. To claim it, reply to the winner’s email with the business name, a short video (up to 30 seconds) or an image, and the link the ad should open. We may decline ads that are not suitable for a family audience. The prize has no cash value and cannot be exchanged or transferred.

5. Fair play

We review winning runs before awarding the prize. Runs made with modified apps, automation or any other form of cheating are disqualified, and the next runner on the board becomes champion. Our decision on eligibility is final.

6. Changes

We may change or end the Weekly Cup at any time. Changes are posted on this page and do not affect a Cup already in progress.

7. Not sponsored by Apple or Google

Apple Inc. and Google LLC are not sponsors of, and are not involved in, the Weekly Cup in any way.

Questions? Contact us at ceo@madrasago.com.
